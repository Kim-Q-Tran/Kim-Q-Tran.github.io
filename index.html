About

Designing bio-inspired materials with mechanics and machine learning.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electromechanical Engineering at Ghent University, Belgium, in the Applied Mechanics Research Group of the Soete Laboratory, supervised by Prof. Magd Abdel Wahab and Prof. Hung Nguyen-Xuan. I am also a research assistant at the Center for AI Research (CAIR), VinUniversity, Vietnam. My doctoral research develops climate-resilient, wave-energy-harvesting breakwater structures inspired by nature.

My work sits where computational mechanics meets machine learning. I build isogeometric and higher-order plate models for bio-inspired porous materials: triply periodic minimal surface (TPMS) lattices and their rotated (RotTPMS) and nano-reinforced variants. I pair these models with probabilistic, physics-informed and explainable AI for uncertainty quantification and inverse design.

Before Ghent, I earned my B.Eng. (rank 3/657) and M.Eng. (rank 1/41) in Civil Engineering at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT, VNU-HCM), and I have been a researcher at the CIRTech Institute, HUTECH, since 2023, with research visits to Ghent University and Ruhr University Bochum.

Research interests

  • Nature- and bio-inspired materials, porous materials and metamaterials
  • Theories and numerical methods in computational mechanics
  • Data-driven and scientific machine learning in computational mechanics
  • Generative and explainable AI in structural and material design
  • Materials design: homogenization, structural and topology optimization, inverse design
  • Sustainable, climate-resilient structures and energy harvesting
